## Overview

I developed a complete front-end project for an Ice Cream brand website, consisting of over 27 manually coded pages built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The project emphasizes responsive design, clean and reusable code, and modern UIs tailored for an e-commerce experience.

## Key features

- 3 unique Home Page designs
- 3 variations of Products Page
- Product Details Page with Add-to-Cart functionality
- Shopping Cart & Checkout pages
- Professional About Us and Contact Us pages
- Multiple Blog Pages
- Dedicated Offers & Discounts page
- Sections for CTA, Newsletter, and Service Features
- Fully responsive and reusable codebase
- Built without external libraries (Pure HTML/CSS/JS)
